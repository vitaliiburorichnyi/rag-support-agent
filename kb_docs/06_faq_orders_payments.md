# FAQ — Orders & Payments

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
Orders can be changed or canceled within 1 hour of placing them, before they enter fulfillment. After that, the order is locked for shipping and must go through the standard return process once delivered.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not currently support buy-now-pay-later services.

**Why was my card declined?**
The most common causes are an incorrect billing address (must match the card statement exactly), insufficient funds, or the bank flagging the transaction as unusual. We recommend the customer contact their bank first; we cannot see the specific decline reason on our end.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes, gift wrapping is a free option at checkout. It includes tissue paper, a ribbon, and a blank card for a personal message.

**Can I use multiple discount codes on one order?**
No, only one discount code can be applied per order. If a customer has two valid codes, they can use the higher-value one.

**Do you price match?**
We don't price match against other retailers. If an item drops in price within 7 days of the customer's purchase, we'll refund the difference as store credit — the customer needs to contact support with their order number.

**I received a discount code but it's not working. What do I do?**
Ask for the exact code and check: expiration date, minimum spend requirement, and whether it's restricted to specific products (some codes exclude final-sale items). If none of those explain it, escalate to a human — there may be a backend issue with the code itself.

**Do you charge sales tax / VAT?**
VAT is included in the displayed price for all EU orders. For non-EU orders, taxes and duties are calculated separately and may be collected by the carrier on delivery.
