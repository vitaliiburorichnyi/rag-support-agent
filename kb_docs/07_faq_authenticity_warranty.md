# FAQ — Authenticity & Warranty

**Are Hush bags handmade?**
Bags are made in small production runs by partner workshops in Portugal and Italy, combining machine stitching for structural seams with hand-finishing on edges, hardware attachment, and quality checks.

**What's your warranty policy?**
All bags carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects — stitching failures, hardware breaking under normal use, or lining separating from the shell. This does not cover general wear and tear, stains, or damage from misuse (e.g., overloading a bag beyond its intended capacity).

**How do I make a warranty claim?**
Contact support with the order number, a description of the issue, and photos. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no cost; if the same style is no longer available, we offer store credit or a comparable alternative.

**Is this bag genuine leather?**
Yes — all leather styles (Mini Tote, City Satchel, Weekender) use full-grain or top-grain leather, listed on each product page. Canvas and suede styles are noted as such and are not leather.

**Someone is selling a "Hush" bag on a marketplace for much less — is it real?**
We only sell directly through our website and a short list of authorized retailers (listed on our About page). We can't verify authenticity for items purchased elsewhere and recommend caution with third-party marketplace listings priced significantly below retail.

**What should I do if I suspect I received a counterfeit?**
This is treated as a priority case — escalate immediately with order number and photos rather than attempting to resolve it as a standard return.
