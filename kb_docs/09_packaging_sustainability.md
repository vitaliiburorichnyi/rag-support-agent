# Packaging & Sustainability

## Packaging materials
Bags ship in a recycled cardboard box with a cotton dust bag included inside (no plastic bag). Padding is made from recycled paper, not bubble wrap or foam.

## Returning packaging
Customers don't need to keep original packaging for a return — only the dust bag and tags need to accompany the returned item.

## Sourcing
Leather is sourced from tanneries certified by the Leather Working Group (LWG), which audits water use, chemical handling, and waste management. Canvas is undyed or low-impact dyed cotton.

## Carbon offset
Every order includes a carbon-neutral shipping offset at no extra cost to the customer, purchased through a third-party offset provider at checkout.

## Repair over replace
Customers can send in a damaged bag for a paid repair assessment even outside the warranty period, rather than defaulting to a new purchase. Repair costs are quoted case-by-case after the item is inspected.

## Recycling old bags
We don't currently run a take-back or recycling program for old bags, including non-Hush items. If a customer asks, direct them to local textile recycling options rather than promising a Hush-run program.
