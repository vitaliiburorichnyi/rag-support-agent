# Product Care — Leather Bags

Applies to: full-grain leather and top-grain leather styles (Mini Tote, City Satchel, Weekender).

## Daily care
Wipe down with a soft, dry cloth after use to remove dust and surface oils. Avoid letting the bag sit in direct sunlight for extended periods — leather fades and can dry out.

## Conditioning
Condition the leather every 3-6 months using a leather-specific conditioner (not a household oil or polish). Apply a small amount to a soft cloth, work it in with circular motions, then let it absorb for 10-15 minutes before buffing off any excess.

## Water exposure
Leather is water-resistant but not waterproof. If the bag gets wet, blot (don't rub) with a dry cloth immediately and let it air dry away from direct heat. Do not use a hairdryer or place it near a radiator — this can cause the leather to crack.

## Stains
For light surface stains, a leather cleaner formulated for the specific leather type works best. We recommend testing any product on a small hidden area first. For ink, oil, or set-in stains, we recommend a professional leather cleaning service rather than DIY treatment, since incorrect products can permanently discolor the leather.

## Storage
Store the bag in its dust bag when not in use, stuffed lightly with tissue paper to help it hold its shape. Avoid hanging leather bags by thin straps for long periods, which can stretch and distort the strap over time.

## Hardware
Metal hardware (zippers, clasps, feet) can be wiped with a dry cloth. Avoid contact with perfume, hairspray, or alcohol-based products, which can tarnish the finish.
