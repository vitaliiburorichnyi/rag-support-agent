# Hush Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The bag must be unused, with all original tags and dust bag included, and show no signs of wear.

Final-sale items (marked as such on the product page) cannot be returned or exchanged.

## How to start a return
Customers use the self-serve return portal linked in their order confirmation email, or reply to support with their order number and reason for return. We issue a prepaid return label for EU orders; international customers cover return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

## Refund timing
Once the returned item is received and inspected (2-3 business days), the refund is issued to the original payment method. Banks typically take an additional 3-7 business days to show the refund on the statement.

## Exchanges
We don't process direct exchanges. The customer returns the original item for a refund and places a new order for the size or color they want. This avoids the delay of waiting for the return to arrive before shipping a replacement.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, the customer gets a full refund or free replacement — no return shipping cost, no 30-day window restriction. We ask for a photo of the damage/defect and the order number to process this immediately without waiting for the item back.

## Wrong item received
If the wrong item was shipped, we send the correct item immediately and provide a prepaid label to return the incorrect one. No cost to the customer either way.

## Gift returns
Items received as gifts can be returned for store credit (not a refund to the original payment method) if the customer has the order number or gift receipt.
