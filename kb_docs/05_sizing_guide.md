# Hush Sizing & Product Dimensions Guide

## Mini Tote
22cm (W) x 16cm (H) x 8cm (D). Fits a phone, cardholder, keys, and a small cosmetics pouch. Not recommended for A5 notebooks or larger. Leather, adjustable strap drop of 15-20cm.

## City Satchel
28cm (W) x 20cm (H) x 10cm (D). Fits a phone, wallet, keys, an A5 notebook, and a small water bottle in the side pocket. Leather, fixed shoulder strap.

## Weekender
50cm (W) x 30cm (H) x 22cm (D). Carries roughly 2-3 days of clothing, fits under most airline seats but may exceed some budget-carrier cabin limits — customers should check their airline's exact dimensions. Leather with canvas lining.

## Everyday Tote
38cm (W) x 32cm (H) x 12cm (D). Fits a 13-15" laptop in the padded interior sleeve, plus a notebook, water bottle, and daily essentials. Canvas with leather trim.

## Market Bag
40cm (W) x 35cm (H) x 15cm (D), unstructured. Best for grocery runs, beach days, or as an overflow bag — not padded, no laptop sleeve. Canvas.

## Evening Clutch
24cm (W) x 14cm (H) x 4cm (D). Fits a phone, cards, lipstick, and keys. Suede with a detachable wrist strap.

## General sizing notes
All dimensions are measured at the widest point of the bag. Strap drop is measured from the top of the bag to the top of the strap when worn on the shoulder, not stretched. For customers asking "will X fit," compare against the item's largest dimension listed above rather than assuming based on bag category alone.
