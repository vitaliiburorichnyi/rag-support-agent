# Hush Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping (EU)
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM CET ship the same day. Cost is €4.90, free on orders over €120.

Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs €12.90 and is available for all EU destinations.

## International shipping
Outside the EU, standard delivery takes 7-14 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Cost is calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Express international (3-5 business days) is available at checkout for most countries.

Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the order total.

## Order tracking
A tracking link is emailed automatically once the order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the carrier scans the package.

## Delays
If a package hasn't moved in the tracking system for more than 5 business days (domestic) or 10 business days (international), contact support with the order number and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

Weather events, customs backlogs, and carrier strikes can extend the above windows. We'll proactively email affected customers when we're aware of a systemic delay.

## Address changes
Address changes are only possible before the order ships. Once a tracking number exists, the address cannot be changed — the customer will need to contact the carrier directly or arrange a redirect through the carrier's own service if available.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer says they haven't received it, we ask them to check with neighbors and the building's parcel room first, then wait 48 hours before we file a claim with the carrier. If tracking has been stalled with no movement for the delay windows above, we open a lost-package claim directly.
