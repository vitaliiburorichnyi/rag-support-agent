# Product Care — Canvas & Suede Bags

Applies to: canvas styles (Everyday Tote, Market Bag) and suede styles (Evening Clutch).

## Canvas care
Spot-clean with a damp cloth and mild soap for small marks. For deeper cleaning, a soft-bristle brush with a small amount of fabric cleaner works well — always air dry flat, never in a dryer, since heat can shrink or warp the canvas structure and any leather trim.

Canvas bags with leather trim or straps should follow leather care guidance for those parts (see Product Care — Leather Bags).

## Suede care
Suede is more delicate than leather or canvas. Use a suede brush to gently lift dirt and restore the nap (the brushed texture) — brush in one direction, not back and forth. Never use water or standard leather cleaners on suede; both can leave permanent watermarks.

For stains, a suede eraser or dry cleaning is the safest option. We recommend professional cleaning for anything beyond light surface dust.

## Weather protection
A suede or canvas protectant spray applied before first use helps resist light rain and stains. Reapply every few months with regular use. Test on a hidden area first.

## Storage
Both materials should be stored in the dust bag, away from direct sunlight and humidity. Suede in particular should not be stored in plastic, which traps moisture and can cause mildew.
